COMPUTED DATA -- FORMULAS REFERENCE
=====================================

This document describes every value in processed/computed/: what it
means, exactly how it is calculated, and which source file it comes
from. Every number in processed/computed/*.csv traces back to a formula
listed here.

Each formula is implemented as a function in functions.py, so the
formula documented here and the code that produces it are the same
thing.


childcare_summary.csv
------------------------

child_development_centers -- compute_childcare_summary()

Count of rows in care_supply_facilities.csv where major_division equals
"Childcare", the facility has valid coordinates, and category contains
"Child Development" (case-insensitive) -- the same match used to color
Child Development Center markers on the map.

This figure counts facilities present in care_supply_facilities.csv with
mappable coordinates. It is not necessarily identical to any external
DSWD licensing roster.


senior_summary.csv
---------------------

compute_senior_summary() draws on two different sources, one row each,
identified in the "source" column:

  metric               formula                                source
  ------               -------                                ------
  registered_seniors   sum(seniors_registered) across all     OSCA administrative
                        barangays                              registration
                                                                (demographics_by_barangay.csv)
  female                sum(age_60plus_f)                     2020 Census
                                                                (demographics_by_barangay.csv)
  male                  sum(age_60plus_m)                     2020 Census
  age_60_79             sum(age_60plus) - sum(age_80plus)      2020 Census
  age_80_plus            sum(age_80plus)                       2020 Census

These figures do not sum to the same total by design. OSCA's registry is
cumulative -- a senior who has since died or moved away remains on the
count -- and reflects a different reference year than the 2020 Census.
The two figures are complementary, not reconcilable: registration data
describes administrative enrollment, while census data describes the
resident population. See the note on the dashboard's Older Persons &
Senior Citizens page for the same explanation shown to dashboard users.


demand_district_context.csv
-------------------------------

load_demand_context() -- district, seniors_registered, and pwd_registered
are read directly from demographics_by_district.csv, ensuring the two
figures always agree.

demand_city_context.csv is a separate, manually-maintained file. It
contains city-level-only administrative data (OSCA seniors-with
-disability overlap, PWD counts by disability type) with no barangay or
district breakdown to derive it from.


facility_counts_and_ratios.csv
----------------------------------

FACILITY COUNTS -- compute_facility_counts_by_barangay(). Counts rows in
care_supply_facilities.csv by barangay, using the file's own "barangay"
column rather than the coordinate-filtered dataframes used for map
rendering, since a facility's barangay assignment does not depend on
whether it has mappable coordinates.

  Column                                          major_division counted
  ------                                          -----------------------
  Childcare                                       Childcare
  Health centers                                  Health centers
  Long-term care and rehabilitation services       Long-term care and rehabilitation services
  Older persons care                              Older persons care
  Quezon City satellite offices for services       Quezon City satellite offices for services
  Schools                                          Schools, deduplicated by (barangay, name) --
                                                    see note below
  Trainings                                        No corresponding division in care_supply_facilities.csv; fixed at 0
  Bus stops                                        See "Bus stop counts" below
  Total                                            Sum of the above, excluding Bus stops

Schools are recorded in care_supply_facilities.csv as one row per grade
level offered -- a school running Preschool, Elementary, and Junior High
programs appears as three rows sharing the same name and location.
Counting rows directly would count that school three times, so School
rows are deduplicated by (barangay, name) before counting, giving one
count per physical school regardless of how many grade levels it offers.

RATIOS -- compute_facility_ratios(). For every entry in
ACCESSIBILITY_RATIO_INDICATORS (functions.py):

    ratio_col = facility_count[facility_col] / demographics[pop_col] * 1000

ACCESSIBILITY_RATIO_INDICATORS is the single source of truth for every
ratio's formula. The table below is a readable rendering of that
definition.

  ratio_col                          facility_col                     pop_col
  ---------                          ------------                     -------
  ratio_childcare (+_f/_m)           Childcare                        age_0_5 (+_f/_m)
  ratio_school_3_5 (+_f/_m)          Schools                          age_3_5 (+_f/_m)
  ratio_school_6_17 (+_f/_m)         Schools                          age_6_17 (+_f/_m)
  ratio_old_60 (+_f/_m)              Older persons care               age_60plus (+_f/_m)
  ratio_old_80 (+_f/_m)              Older persons care               age_80plus (+_f/_m)
  ratio_pwd                          Long-term care and               pwd_registered
                                      rehabilitation services
  ratio_pop_health (+_f/_m)          Health centers                   pop_census (+ pop_female/pop_male)
  ratio_child_health (+_f/_m)        Health centers                   age_0_5 (+_f/_m)
  ratio_old_health (+_f/_m)          Health centers                   age_60plus (+_f/_m)
  ratio_pwd_health                   Health centers                   pwd_registered
  ratio_pwd_all                      Total                            pwd_registered
  ratio_bus_stops (+_f/_m)           Bus stops                        pop_census (+ pop_female/pop_male)


Bus stop counts
-------------------

compute_bus_stop_counts_by_barangay(). Uses care_supply_facilities.csv's
own "barangay" value for a bus stop where one is present. For any row
without one, barangay is assigned via a point-in-polygon spatial join of
its coordinates against qc_barangays.geojson.


demographics_by_district.csv
--------------------------------

compute_demographics_by_district(). Aggregates
demographics_by_barangay.csv to district level using two methods,
depending on the column:

  1. Summable columns (population, age bands, seniors/PWD registrations,
     migrant workers, CBMS coverage) are summed directly across each
     district's barangays.

  2. Derived columns (density, ratios, rates, shares, CBMS percentages)
     are recomputed from the district-level sums, not averaged from
     barangay-level values.

CBMS indicators are computed under two weighting schemes:
_hhw (weighted by cbms_responding_hh, so each barangay contributes in
proportion to the amount of CBMS data it reported) and _popw (weighted
by pop_census, so each resident contributes equally, consistent with
every other derived variable in this table).


Socio-economic (CBMS) indicators -- methodology
----------------------------------------------------

Sourced from the demand-side data pipeline and its accompanying
codebook. CBMS 2024 is a secondary source -- household-reported,
covering approximately 71% of the census population citywide -- and its
indicators are calculated only against CBMS's own reporting base, never
against a census denominator.

  Column                                  Formula
  ------                                  -------
  cbms_avg_household_size                 Total population covered by
                                           CBMS-responding households /
                                           number of responding households.
  cbms_avg_nuclear_families_per_hh        Total nuclear families reported
                                           by responding households / number
                                           of responding households. Values
                                           above 1 indicate multiple families
                                           sharing one household.
  cbms_food_insecurity_prevalence_pct     Households worried about food /
                                           total responding households * 100.
  cbms_food_severe_wholeday_pct           Households reporting a whole day
                                           without eating / total responding
                                           households * 100 -- the most
                                           severe food-insecurity band.
  cbms_food_intensity_score               sum(item_count * severity_rank) /
                                           sum(item_counts), rank 1 (worried)
                                           to 8 (whole day without eating).
                                           Bounded 1-8 by construction.
  cbms_housing_inadequacy_index_pct       Mean of unimproved-roof,
                                           unimproved-wall, and
                                           unimproved-floor percentages.
  cbms_housing_makeshift_severe_pct       Maximum of makeshift/salvaged
                                           -material percentage across
                                           roof, wall, and floor.
  pop_cbms_secondary                      Total population covered by
                                           CBMS-responding households in
                                           the barangay. A secondary
                                           population figure, not used as
                                           a ratio denominator.

Damar (District 1) has no CBMS 2024 data; it is absent from the raw CBMS
source table rather than recorded as zero. It remains fully represented
in every census-based and administrative indicator.
